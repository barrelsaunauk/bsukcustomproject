Your brief

Nine apartments, one sauna

Gwel y Mor is nine holiday apartments above Bull Bay on the Anglesey coast. Here is what you asked us for.

Who uses it
Shared by guests
Frequent, unsupervised use by different guest parties, so it has to be simple to run and solid enough to take it.
How many
Four to six people

Exposed coast

Exclusive slots
Each apartment given its own booked time, so the door needs to lock and the sauna needs to be hot when the slot starts.

Your questions

Nine questions, nine answers

In the order you asked them.

👥
1. Model and capacity
Both seat up to six

Both options are built for guest use. The cube gives you a straight sided room with a covered terrace; the barrel is our handcrafted signature build. Same heater, same seating, same price.

🌊
2. Exposed coastal conditions
Thermowood and stainless steel

Treated thermowood is heat dried at 200°C for the outdoors, and every band, fixing and hinge is stainless steel. On a coastal site salt in the air accelerates wear, so rinse the exterior occasionally with fresh water and oil it twice a year rather than once. Our aftercare guide sets it out.

⚡
3. Electrical supply
Your electrician connects it

The heater comes with the build. The wiring is done by your own qualified electrician to BS 7671, and the heater's rating decides whether you need a single phase or three phase supply. James confirms the heater model and its rating with you, so your electrician has the figure before anything is ordered.

🚚
4. Delivery and installation to Bull Bay
Send us your access route

We deliver and install across the UK and Ireland. Fully built and lifted into place by crane or forklift, or built on site by our team where access is tight. Send us a short video of your access route and we come back with the method, the cost and the timescale.

🔐
5. Access control for booked slots
Lockable as standard

Both builds come with a lockable door and spare keys. A keypad or code lock in place of the key, so each apartment has its own code for its slot, is something we can price for you; ask James on your call. The WiFi heater helps here too: set a timer and the sauna is hot when the slot starts.

🛡️
6. Warranty and servicing for guest use
24 months, structural

Structural components carry a 24 month warranty from delivery or completion of installation. For commercial and multi user settings, holiday lets included, cover is for clear structural defects; cosmetic wear from guest use is not covered. Servicing is a yearly routine: full inspection, an electrics check by your electrician, band tightening and a deep clean inside and out, with the exterior oiled twice a year on the coast.

📋
7. Planning permission
Usually permitted development

Saunas generally sit under permitted development, but it is site dependent, and a holiday let is a business use. Isle of Anglesey County Council is the authority for Bull Bay. James can talk you through what other holiday let owners have done.

🧾
8. Does the price include VAT
No, VAT is added

Both prices are £10,000 plus VAT. Your estimate price excludes VAT, stated separately on your itemised estimate.

🏷️
9. Ex display models
Made to order

We do not hold much stock. Everything is made to order, though the odd unit is built up between jobs. James will tell you what is on the floor when you speak, and if you like one we can make it again to your specification.
