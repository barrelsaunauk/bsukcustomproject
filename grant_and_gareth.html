The ice bath station

Three ways to lay out your ice baths. Every layout comes with a chiller and filter unit, and lights inside every tub.

Option 1
Built in ice bath deck
  • Three ice baths set into a raised timber box
  • Only the rims show above the top
  • One short flight of steps up to the baths
Option 2
Modern black frame station
  • Slim black steel frame with timber slat screens
  • Solar panels across the whole roof
  • Three single tubs, each with its own step
🧩
Mix and matchYou have the option to combine elements of each. The built in deck with the black frame, the standing baths under a solar roof, whatever suits your guests. We will build it to suit.
Option 3
Three standing ice baths
  • Three single person barrel ice baths
  • Each with its own timber step
  • Open decking around every bath
