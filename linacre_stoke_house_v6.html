1. One supplier
One workshop, one package

The sauna, the Double Dipper and both single dippers are built in the same Welsh workshop, by the same team, and priced together as one package.

⚡
2. Electrical supply
Your electrician connects it

The heater comes with the build. The wiring is done by your own qualified electrician to BS 7671, and the heater's rating decides whether you need a single phase or three phase supply. James confirms the heater model and its rating with you, so your electrician has the figure before anything is ordered.

🚚
3. Delivery and installation to Stoke House
Send us your access route

We deliver and install across the UK and Ireland. Fully built and lifted into place by crane or forklift, or built on site by our team where access is tight. Send us a short video of your access route and we come back with the method, the cost and the timescale.

🛡️
4. Warranty and servicing for shared use
24 months, structural

Structural components carry a 24 month warranty from delivery or completion of installation. For commercial and multi user settings, cover is for clear structural defects; cosmetic wear from shared use is not covered. Servicing is a yearly routine: full inspection, an electrics check by your electrician, band tightening and a deep clean inside and out.

🧾
5. Does the price include VAT
No, VAT is added

The package is £39,985 plus VAT. Your estimate price excludes VAT, stated separately on your itemised estimate.

🚿
6. Showers and decking
Can be added to your package

The decking platform can be extended to incorporate other products from our range, creating a complete outdoor wellness space: thermowood ice baths, barrel showers and outdoor seating areas. James can price these with you.
