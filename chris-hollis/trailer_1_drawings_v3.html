Which width are we building?

This is the one decision holding your build back. The interior, finishes and decor can all be settled later. Jarek needs the width before he can start your frame.

A properly insulated wall takes up room on both sides. Each wall is built up like this:

100 insulated wall40 OSB, both faces40 battens, both faces20 air gap+ your chosen cladding

That is around 200 mm of wall each side, so you lose about 400 mm of width inside.

2.4 m outside

About 2.0 m of usable width inside once the walls are in.

Our recommendation

2.6 m outside

About 2.2 m inside. The extra 200 mm makes a real difference to the bed, the ensuite and moving around.

Please reply in the group with 2.4 or 2.6.If we do not have your answer this week, we will build to our standard Rowan size so your place in the workshop is kept.
