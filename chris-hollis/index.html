Before we build

Points to raise

Open questions between Chris's design pack, his planning drawing and what the workshop is building. Updated 23 Nov 2026.

1

Overall height and the raised front roofUrgent

Chris's design pack asks for a raised front roof that lifts over the pergola beam. Drawn to his heights, the hut came out at about 3590 to the roof crown. His planning drawing (1557:001) shows a standard curved roof at 3015 overall. With the measured louvres it drops to about 3480, still above that.

Chris: is the raised roof covered by your planning approval, or do we keep to 3015?
2

Hut frame sizeUrgent

Three sizes are in play. Design pack 5500 × 2400. Planning drawing 5600 × 2470. Agreed build: body 2600 wide (floor and walls) on the 2400 chassis, overhanging 100 each side.

Jarek: confirm the final body size before the walls go up.
3

Door, pergola beam and louvresAnswered in part

Louvre clearance now measured by Chris at 90 (19 Nov), replacing the 200 allowance. With 50 clear above that, the roof edge sits at about 3190 and the crown at about 3480. Still open: the design pack has the beam only 130 above the door head, and the workshop needs about 200 for the door frame and roof overhang.

Jarek: confirm the door head height so the beam clears it.
4

Pergola make and sizeNeeded soon

The design pack shows a 4 × 3 m louvred pergola. A 4 × 4 m Bulhawk was also linked on 13 Oct.

Chris: make, model and size, ideally the maker's data sheet.
5

Wall height and floor levelNeeded soon

The design pack has a 1950 wall with a 750 × 1950 stable door, which leaves no room for the door frame. Door threshold is 750 in the pack and 675 on the planning drawing. Deck, step and bath heights all follow from this.

Jarek: wall height and floor level you are building to.
6

Deck sizeTo confirm

Drawn at 4300 × 3150 from the design pack. Our estimate describes a deck the length of the hut, running five metres out.

Chris: which deck are we pricing?
7

Bath and compact saunaTo confirm

Bath drawn as a 1500 × 1050 opening in a 1800 × 1250 surround. Sauna drawn 1800 long, the pack shows 2000, on a taller frame than our standard one.

Chris: make, model and size of the bath you are supplying.
8

Inside layoutTo confirm

The design pack has two interior layouts, V1 and V2.

Chris: which interior are we building?
Deck supports, timber sizes and pile depth are allowances, confirmed by our workshop and the site survey.
