Your spec

The full build, room by room. We go through every detail with you on your spec call.

The cabin

  • Timber walls with stainless steel tightening bands
  • Curved roof finished in bitumen shingles
  • 28mm pine floorboards
  • Timber side door with a double glazed window, timber handle and lock

The sauna

  • Wood fired stove, loaded from outside
  • Tempered tinted glass door between the sauna and the sitting space
  • Tempered double glazed end window
  • Two benches, angled or straight, in Aspen or Black Alder
  • Two portable foot rests with the angled benches
  • Heat resistant plates around the stove

The hot tub

  • Stainless steel wood fired heater, fed from outside
  • 2m chimney with its protection
  • 12 hydro massage jets
  • 12 air jets on a 750W pump
  • 3 in 1 controller for air, jets and lights
  • 15 LED star lights
  • Hard wearing surround, steps and a drain valve
  • Electrical fuse safety box

Headrests and a folding cover

Soft headrests round the tub and a cover that folds back in one move, so the water stays warm between guests.
